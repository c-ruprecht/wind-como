Breva in the next 7 days

Model rows: chance of a Breva day (at least 2 h in a row with 14+ kn on the upper lake) and of a long Breva (at least 5 h – typically stable high pressure with a strong gradient from the plain into the valleys), from the Breva model fed with the ECMWF forecast. Breva signs: south wind over the lake in the model, pressure gradient from the Po plain into the valleys, sun in the valleys, no north foehn, dry air; rain dampens. “Usual”: share of Breva days in the same month. How to read the %: the chances are calibrated on real forecasts 2024–2026 – 55 % means that on 55 of 100 days with such a forecast there really was a Breva. Click a day for the hours.

Weather models: Open-Meteo (ICON, ECMWF). Measurements: ARPA Lombardia (dati.lombardia.it), 10-minute values. Breva map: Copernicus Sentinel-1, own processing (100 m, CMOD5.N).
